# Nexus Nepal · E-Commerce Platform & Himalayan Marketplace

An authentic Himalayan marketplace and modern commerce web application for Nepal, combining high-craftsmanship heritage goods, organic teas, trekking gear, and electronics with a signature animated dual-panel authentication system.

---

## Key Features

1. **Signature Dual-Panel Auth Experience**
   - Slanted diagonal split-card design with mountain ridge line artwork.
   - Smooth switching between **Sign In** and **Create Account**.
   - Interactive password strength meter with 4-level indicator.
   - "Keep me signed in" toggle & show/hide password buttons.
   - Clean authentication with input validation.

2. **Himalayan Artisan & Products Catalog**
   - **Himalayan Crafts:** Pure Mustang Pashmina Cashmere, Handcrafted 7-Metal Singing Bowl Set, Hand-Cast Brass Diya, Boudhanath Shakyamuni Buddha Thangka Canvas.
   - **Organic & Foods:** Certified Organic Ilam Orthodox Tea, Wild Himalayan Cliff Honey (Annapurna harvest), Organic Gundruk, Spicy Dalle Khursani & Timmur Aachar.
   - **Apparel & Weaves:** Handwoven Palpali Dhaka Topi, Dhaka Scarf/Muffler.
   - **Mountain Gear & Lifestyle:** Carbon Trekking Poles Pair, Hammered Ayurvedic Copper Water Bottle.
   - **Modern Essentials & Tech:** Nexus Alpine Pro Smartwatch (Altitude/Barometer), Sonic ANC Wireless Earbuds, Trekker 20000mAh Powerbank.

3. **Nepali Digital Checkout & Logistics**
   - **Currency:** Nepali Rupees (`NPR` / `Rs.`).
   - **Payment Integrations:** eSewa, Khalti Digital Wallet, Cash on Delivery (COD), connectIPS.
   - **Delivery across Nepal:** Express delivery for Kathmandu Valley and shipping across all 7 Provinces (77 districts).
   - **Promotions:** Free delivery on orders over Rs. 4,000, promo codes (`HIMALAYA10`, `NEXUS500`).

4. **Account & Tracking**
   - User profile with Himalayan Club reward points.
   - Live order status tracking (`Processing`, `Dispatched from Kathmandu Hub`, `Out for Delivery`, `Delivered`).
   - Order history and saved addresses in Nepal.
